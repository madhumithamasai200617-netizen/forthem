PREVENTION GUIDE

Build your prevention mindset.

Simple actions can help create stronger habits, healthier relationships and better decision-making.

01

Know your triggers

Become aware of situations, emotions or environments that may influence risky decisions.

02

Build healthy routines

Physical activity, hobbies, meaningful goals and positive relationships can strengthen wellbeing.

03

Learn to say no

A clear and confident response can help you maintain your boundaries when facing pressure.

04

Ask for support

Reaching out to someone you trust can be an important step when a situation feels difficult.
