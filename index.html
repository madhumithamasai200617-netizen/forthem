WHY FOR THEM?

Prevention starts with awareness.

Substance abuse can affect individuals, families, education, careers and communities. Awareness helps people recognize risks, understand consequences and make healthier decisions.

🧠

Knowledge

Understanding the risks and consequences of substance abuse can help people make informed decisions and protect their future.

💪

Strength

Real strength is having the confidence to protect your goals, health and future even when facing pressure.

❤️

Support

Nobody should feel alone. Finding trusted support can be an important step toward a healthier and stronger life.

OUR MISSION

Building a generation that chooses life.

FOR THEM aims to make prevention education engaging, accessible and supportive. Instead of simply telling people what not to do, we want to help them understand why their future is worth protecting.
