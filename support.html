YOUR SUPPORT NETWORK

Who can you talk to?

You don't need to figure everything out by yourself. Consider reaching out to someone who makes you feel safe and heard.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family

A parent, guardian, sibling or another family member may be able to listen, support you and help you find further help.

🧑‍🏫

Teacher or Mentor

A trusted teacher, mentor or counselor can help you talk through a difficult situation and identify possible options.

🧑‍⚕️

Healthcare Professional

Healthcare professionals can provide appropriate assessment, guidance and referrals when needed.
